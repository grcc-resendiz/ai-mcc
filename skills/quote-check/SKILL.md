# Quote Check

When someone asks you to check a quote, run these five checks in order. Mark each one PASS or FIX.

1. Prices: every line matches the price list. Anything not on the price list says "Not on the price list, needs an estimate from engineering" and is left out of the total.
2. Customer requirements: every requirement for that customer is applied. Customer A: stainless steel frame, stainless guarding, and a washdown rated motor. Customer B: a recommended spare parts kit. Customer C: Net 45 terms.
3. Standard items: the quote includes one motor and gearbox and 8 hours of engineering and drawings.
4. Math: each line total is quantity times unit price, the subtotal adds up, the surcharge is 5 percent of the subtotal, and the total is the subtotal plus the surcharge. Show your math. If any number on the quote doesn't match your math, mark Math FIX.
5. Terms: valid for 30 days, lead time of 8 to 10 weeks from approved drawings, freight and installation quoted separately, and the right payment terms.

Report it like this:
A table with three columns: Check, Result, What to fix.
Then one line: "Ready to send" if all five pass, or "Fix before sending" and the number of fixes.

Rules:
Only report. Never change a price, approve a discount, or send anything.
If the quote is missing information you need for a check, mark that check FIX and say what's missing.
